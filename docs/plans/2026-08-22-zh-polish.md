**Goal:** Fix the three zh-locale text families from the 2026-08-22 render exam: Z1 rate-unit mix (`/分` vs `/min` vs `/MIN`), Z3 share-chip denominator contradicting the card total by one cent, and Z4 CJK composition (dangling interpunct, mid-word subtitle gap).

No new dependencies, no layout-engine changes.

**Tech Stack:** React 19 + TS, vitest (jsdom), Playwright e2e, bun.

## Rulings (made under delegated judgment; audited 2026-08-22 by a fresh Opus pass — all upheld)

- **R1 — English HUD stays.** `BLUEPRINT VIEW`, `STATUS · READY`, `SECTOR-01…`, `SET · 01`, `SUP · 02`, `LOOP`, `PACK ·`/`REV ·` are deliberate sci-fi chrome, consistent across shell and canvas; the exam itself flagged them "possibly intentional". Out of scope; recorded here so the exam family can be closed with a ruling.
- **R2 — Card total is the source of truth (Z3).** This *reverses* the PR #51 display-total additivity policy for share denominators: they now format the exact trunk total (matching every card), so member chips may visibly sum to one cent off the denominator. The exam ruled the chip-vs-card contradiction is the real accounting-error read; tooltips keep exact rates either way. Audit confirmed no hidden same-edge contradiction: the drop chip renders only on single-member trunks, where quantized and exact format identically. `busDisplayTotalRate`, its accumulators, and `quantizeRateToDisplay` (whose only remaining caller was busRouting) are deleted.
- **R3 — Z4c zh truncation deferred.** Long-CJK tail-ellipsis (`砂叶粉末` rows, `据点供货`) is the F3 mechanism; a disambiguation-aware elision is its own fix and stays with the F3 family.
- **R4 — Caption casing kept.** `.pn-kind`'s `text-transform: uppercase` (`canvas.css:1886`) stays: a no-op on Han; en keeps its `OUT · TARGET · 120/MIN` look with the localized unit slotted in.
- **R5 — Interpunct clings to the following token.** Separator becomes space + `·` + `\u00A0`: a wrap then yields a leading dot that reads as a list bullet, never a stranded trailing dot.
- **R6 — The `.pn-rate .unit` 3px gap stays.** The exam's "150 /min" space is `margin-left: 3px` (`canvas.css:1911-1918`), a deliberate card style; zh gets the same `150 /分` gap. Only the unit language was filed, and zeroing the margin would change en. No CSS change.
